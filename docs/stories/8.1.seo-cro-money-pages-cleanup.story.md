## Story

**As a** SEO and acquisition owner,
**I want** the site to clean weak indexation signals and strengthen conversion pages,
**so that** planoamilempresas.com.br can concentrate crawl, authority, and organic leads on high-value Amil empresarial searches.

---

## Strategic Context / Why

The 2026-06-30 SEO audit found that the site already has a strong long-tail network asset, but some signals need cleanup before scaling further. The largest immediate risks are sitemap/noindex misalignment on provider pages, overlong title templates, and a short `/cotacao-online` page that does not yet behave like a full bottom-funnel landing page.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

1. `sitemap-prestadores.xml` only lists provider URLs that are intended to be indexable under the existing city-density rule.
2. Site title templating avoids repeated "Plano Amil 2026 | BeneficioRH" suffixes on pages that already include commercial title context.
3. `/cotacao-online` is expanded into a stronger bottom-funnel landing page with trust, process, FAQ, and links to pricing, network, and business segment pages.
4. Commercial capture content preserves the honest compliance posture: no unsupported official-operator claim, clear broker positioning, and no invented price guarantee.
5. Tests or assertions cover the sitemap provider filtering behavior.
6. Standard quality gates pass: `npm run lint`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm test`, and `npm run build`.

---

## Out of Scope

- Creating new slugs such as `/amil-cnpj` or `/amil-pme`; these need a separate canonical/redirect architecture decision.
- Rewriting every city, provider, and blog page in the long-tail network.
- Deploying or pushing to remote; only @devops may handle remote operations.

### Key Technical Context

Provider pages already use `MIN_PRESTADORES_MUNICIPIO_PARA_PRESTADOR_INDIVIDUAL = 5` to mark low-density city provider pages as `noindex,follow` and canonicalize to the city page. The provider sitemap should not list URLs that are intentionally noindex. The title helper currently appends a long commercial suffix to all route titles.
